About Geo Homes Limited

Your Dream Our Responsibility

Geo Homes Limited, formerly known as Geo Properties Limited, is a property development company established in 2007.

Over the years, the company has developed and handed over 18 projects across several important locations in Dhaka, including Uttara, Azimpur, Eskaton, Kalabagan, Hatirpool, Mohammadpur, Rampura, and Dhanmondi.

With a focus on property development and strategically located projects, Geo Homes Limited continues to expand its presence in the Dhaka real estate sectors.
