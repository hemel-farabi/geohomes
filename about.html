About Geo Homes Limited

Building a Legacy of Real Estate & Construction Development

Geo Homes Limited is a real estate development company based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. The company was previously known as Geo Properties Limited and started its operations in 2007.

Since its establishment, Geo Homes Limited has developed and handed over 18 projects in different parts of Dhaka. The company's completed project locations include Uttara, Azimpur, Eskaton, Kalabagan, Hatirpool, Mohammadpur, Rampura, and Dhanmondi.

Today, the company has project and development activities in locations including Bashundhara, Jolshiri, Mohammadpur, Kalabagan, and Eskaton.
